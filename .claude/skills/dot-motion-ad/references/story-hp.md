# Story: the ChatGPT ad → Humanly Possible

## The idea

The ChatGPT ad is the evolution of human progress told by one dot: cell, hunters, fire, coin, wheel, horses, agriculture, sea, ship, train, lightbulb, X-ray, flight, DNA, TV, the moon landing, the network, the chip, and then "now this". HP's thesis fits it exactly:

- Every era, humans got a new tool and more became humanly possible: fire, the wheel, the horse, electricity, flight, DNA, the moon.
- AI is the next tool in that line, not a scary technical thing.
- The payoff: **"With AI, more is now humanly possible."** (Jillian's VO, official wording; Aidan once typed "now is more", but the official line is this one.)
- Then the show's promise: real people telling stories of what they did with it: **"New stories every single week"** (headline, not final; see below) and **"and we'd love to hear where it's taking you."**

Aidan's own framing (11/4): hit hard on the story. We started with the first tools (spear, fire), then riding horses, light bulbs and electricity, planes, DNA, landing on the moon, and now AI, and what we can do with that.
