# The dot concept + episode intros (decided 11/5/2026)

Read this before building ANY Humanly Possible episode intro, teaser or show-open with dots. It is the "why" behind every dot decision from here on.

## What the dots mean

From the call with Kodi Zene (Season 1 website teaser + dot concept, 11/5) and Aidan's own notes:

- **The dots are energy: the life force, the lifeblood of humans, what we're all made of.** They connect everyone and they help build things.
- **NOT cells, NOT biology.** Aidan first pitched "human cells and bodily systems"; Kodi steered it away from that because it reads morbid. Never show the dots as tissue, blood cells, skeletons-as-anatomy or anything clinical. Energy, warmth, connection.
- **The logo is the heart.** The HP mark is where all the energy comes home. Every dot story resolves into the mark (the v1-voiced ending is the reference: dots swoop in from below and fill the mark bottom to top, drift left, solidify, the words come in with Uppit).
- We are "going back to the dot format." The real-footage / AI-generated human versions (v1-real-footage, v1-wes) are shelved for now: stock reads bland, AI footage reads as AI.

Kodi's verbatim framing from the notes: a teaser "to introduce the 'dot' concept as a representation of lifeblood, life force, or cells"; a trailer "where the dots introduce and move items into place"; reframed "as energy and a life force that connects everyone and helps build things."

## The episode format

Aidan's notes (verbatim intent): "these dot animations kind of explain a story... won't reveal anything... I don't know any show that has done this before."

1. **Dot story intro, 30 s to 1 min.** A short story told only in dots that sets up the episode's theme. It **reveals nothing**: no guest name, no face, no punchline, no answer. The viewer feels the theme first.
2. **Jillian's quick intro.** She names the guest and what they reached (the handoff out of the dots into her).
3. **Body.** The conversation.
4. **Outro.** Still open (see below).

## How the teaser relates

The landing-page teaser ("Original with Voice", `video-projects/hp-teaser-pack-2/v1-voiced`, see `teaser-v1-voiced.md`) is the prototype of this language: a dot story about the human journey that ends with the dots coming home to the heart. Each episode intro is a smaller, personal version built around one episode's theme. Reuse its engine, its ending, its motion rules and its craft (v1's original ending craft beat every newer scene we tried; see the r4 lesson).

## Rules for building an episode intro

- All dots, no footage halftones (team feedback 11/4: "the dot creations carried through for all the scenes").
- People = stick rigs (v1 hunter style) or the v2 human-dots style Aidan likes. NO filled-blob humanoids ("look kind of funny").
- Human first: the story is about a person's life getting better, not technology advancing (Kodi + Jillian, round 2).
- Only Jillian's voice: real recordings or the Fish clone (`scripts/fish_tts.py`). Never another TTS voice. Write lines she can re-record (see `teaser-v1-voiced.md` for the VO script PDF pattern).
- Keep it unrevealing: theme, emotion, metaphor. Anything that names or shows the guest belongs to Jillian's intro, not the dot story.
- My recommendation (not yet confirmed by Aidan): end every intro on the dots flowing into the mark so every episode hands off to Jillian the same way, like a heartbeat logo moment.

## Open questions (ask before or while building the first test intro)

- Does each dot story come from the guest's own story, or a broader theme they represent?
- Narrated (Jillian VO) or music only until she comes in?
- Outro: call back to the opening dot story, or a standard close? (Comment left in the standup doc.)
