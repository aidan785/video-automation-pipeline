Organize governance into
Three zones
Zone oneBoundary design
Limits you build into the system itself. The three decisions from the definition.
What the AI may do
Which actions it can take. My agent could post to any channel when it should have sent one DM.
Where the data travels
Which systems and companies it passes through. A patient record leaving the client's environment on its way to a model provider.
Who answers for it
A named person. A system nobody owns is an incident nobody catches.
Yours, the builder. Enforced with permissions and code.
Zone twoStandards translation
NIST AI RMF · ISO 42001 · the EU AI Act. Which parts apply to a client is a specialist's job.
Your job is recognition. Bring in the expert: they say what must be true, you make it true in the build.
Zone threeLegal judgment
Is a specific use of AI lawful for this business, in this place? You never touch zone three. Not a little, not confidently, not when the client pushes.
Handled by lawyers. Your contribution is having spotted the question and sent it there.
