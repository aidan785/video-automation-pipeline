Where people go wrong
Three Scoring Mistakes
01
Treating feasibility as a checkbox"They have the data" is not diligence. Where does it live? How much? What format? Who maintains it?
02
Scoring the whole workflow as one thingYou get a mush answer. The truth lives at the step level.
03
Forgetting the question after "can it"Should it? Some things are feasible and still wrong to automate.
