The AI Control Ladder
Seven rungs, strongest first
Top · impossible · cheapDown · unlikely · costly · needs attention
01
Remove the capabilityBrightwater: exactly three tools. Look up, reschedule, escalate. No payment tool, so writing to payments is impossible.
02
Constrain what it can reachThe scoped API key. Retrieval reaches one patient's appointment and insurance status, not the calendar, chart, or payments.
03
Constrain what it can doThe read/write split. Lookup only reads, so a hallucinated update to a patient record can't happen.
04
Constrain what it can sayThe routing form: category, urgency, route, draft reply, needs-human flag. Anything else is rejected, not executed.

Two rules govern the whole ladder
01
Impossible beats unlikelyA control that makes the failure impossible beats one that makes it unlikely
Same headspace as a contract: assume the worst case up front, write the terms you hope never matter
02
Cost climbs as you go downSeven rungs, strongest first. Lower rungs cost more to build and depend on more going right
Take the highest rung that actually covers the failure you diagnosed
Rung five · gate it with a human
Brightwater's clinical branch: anything clinical goes to a person, every single time, by construction
“I drafted this. Do you want me to send it?” Approve · Reject · Revise, right in Slack. The send tool only fires after a yes.
Mistake one: gating everything. Within a week the human rubber-stamps without reading
Mistake two: gating nothing that matters
Gate by risk: anything that touches a database, anything customer-facing
