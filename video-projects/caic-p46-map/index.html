The row people forget · the trigger
A system trigger (a form, a database event, a schedule): you know exactly what you'll get. Deterministic.
Brightwater's trigger is a human with a keyboard. Completely unpredictable.
A patient will text their whole medical history to a scheduling number, unprompted
!
Add a boundary at the front doorA small, cheap model scrubs identifiers out of the raw text before the capable model ever sees it
May never read: raw, unscrubbed patient free text
And think about the malicious human: an endpoint hit a thousand times a minute, a dollar every run
Is the build governed? Four questions
01
Is every limit enforced by a permission or a deterministic check?A plain code rule that runs the same way every time. Not a prompt.
02
Does every database or customer step have a gate?Or a written reason it doesn't
03
Does every run leave a trace?
04
Is every zone 2 or 3 flag written down?With the name of who it went to
Four yeses: governed. Any no: your to-do list, shorter than you'd think.
