Definition
An eval is a repeatable way of checking whether an AI’s output is good, instead of just trusting your impression of it.
Why you run one, for the client
Three reasons
01
SafetyDid the misses fail safely? Safe: escalates to a human with a log. Unsafe: the wrong email, a deleted record, a cancellation nobody asked for.
Safety comes before the client’s number
02
MoneyThe milestone from engagement design: ninety percent of two hundred cases go where they should, and none grants access or cancels. The eval is the proof that gets you paid.
03
TrustTwo-week updates on a black box. Show the internal QA before they ever see a score.
The eval makes invisible work visible
Employed track: safety is the same, money is the sign-off to ship, trust is your manager’s confidence

An eval does two jobs

QAProve the agent does what the client’s expert said it should

OptimizationSame score at half the cost or twice the speed. Visible only once quality is proven.
“We’re going to evaluate this automation to make sure it’s behaving the way it should, and see what we can do to optimize cost, speed, and performance.”
