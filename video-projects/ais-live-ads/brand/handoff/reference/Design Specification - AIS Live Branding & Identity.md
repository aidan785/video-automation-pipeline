# Design Specification: AIS Live Branding & Identity

## 1\. Project Overview

**Event Name:** AIS Live: Real Projects, Real Revenue   
**The Mission:** AIS Live Logo, with interchangeable taglines depending on the event.

## 2\. Brand North Star: "The Config Aesthetic"

The visual identity must move away from "standard corporate SaaS" and toward the **Dynamic Builder** energy of Figma’s [Config](https://config.figma.com/). I love the colours and movement on this site and it is a great conference. Our logo already has some movement in it \- let’s make it more defined. 

* **Key Visual Logic:** Spatial layering, modular elements, and bold, high-contrast typography.  
* **Core Audience Expansion:** While the current demographic is 95% male (ages 35-45), the branding must feel inclusive and sophisticated enough to attract a broader, diverse range of founders and technical leaders.

## 3\. Visual Language & Palette

We are moving away from the "dark gaming basement" vibe.

* **Primary Anchor:** **Electric Blue** (Essential for AIS brand continuity).  
* **Action Palette:**  
  * **Electric Blue:** Tech, Logic, AI Nodes.  
  * **Cyber Yellow:** Revenue, Success, Call-to-Actions.  
  * **Action Orange:** Energy, "Live" elements, Outdoor activities.  
* **Backgrounds:** Deep Charcoal or Onyx Black to make colors "vibrate."

### Visual References

* **Skeo (Depth & Blue/Black):** [Skeo Reference](https://dribbble.com/Proficientsstudio) \- Specifically for the depth and premium "glow" of the blue.  
* **Evoxa (Social Proof):** [Evoxa Reference](https://dribbble.com/shots/27274840-Evoxa-Brand-Identity-Design) \- For minimalist, high-typography testimonial cards.  
* **Config (Movement & Layering):** [Figma Config](https://config.figma.com/) \- For the "moving blocks" and layered UI feel.  In Figma there are also a LOT of great templates for social ads and sponsorship decks (not sure if we are developing or Smooth Media is developing)  
* [Unbound Conference](https://unbound.hubspot.com/) \- I don’t like the colours, but the shapes, fonts and movement are great \- very recognizable branding.

## 4\. 12-Month Roadmap & Asset Requirements

The branding must be modular to scale from virtual landing pages to physical signage.

### Phase 1: Virtual Events & Lead Gen

* **Landing Page:** Minimalist but high-motion. Use "Spatial UI" where elements overlap and hover.  
* **Social Asset Pack:**  
  * **Speaker Cards:** Layered "3D" frames for headshots.  
  * **Testimonial Templates:** Evoxa-inspired text-only cards.  
  * **Ad Assets:** High-contrast blocks with "REVENUE" in massive, bold typography.

### Phase 2: The Live Event (Physical & Outdoor)

* **Website:** Full event site with interactive elements and activity schedules.  
* **Sponsorship Deck:** Professional, high-impact deck (Smooth Media style) using the "brand colours" or "Config" layering.  
* **On-Site Signage:** High-visibility "Safety Orange" and "Electric Blue" markers for outdoor activities.
