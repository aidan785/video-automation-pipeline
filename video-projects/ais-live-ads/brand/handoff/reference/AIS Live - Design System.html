The mark

03 · Lockup

The wordmark is Montserrat 900 italic, set tight at -0.03em. “AIS” takes the surface’s ink color, “LIVE” takes the primary blue (or its inverse on warm grounds), and a small red live-dot sits at the top-right baseline — the one element that never changes color.

Live signal

04 · Motion

The pulsing red dot is the only ambient motion in the system. It pulses on a 1.6s ease-in-out loop with an outward shadow ring. Animation is killed in print to avoid hard-edged artifacts.

Surfaces & sheets

06 · Layout

Sheets are US Letter (8.5 × 11in), printed full-bleed with the cream / onyx / charcoal grounds carried edge-to-edge. Inner padding is 0.6in; chrome eyebrow + footer rules sit at 0.35in from the edge.
