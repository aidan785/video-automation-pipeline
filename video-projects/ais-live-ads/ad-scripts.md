# **AIS Live — Paid Ads v2 (Pain-Based Hooks)**

Four ad scripts for AIS Live, read by Nate to camera. One hook per pain point. Each ad \= Hook (variable) \+ Standard Body \+ CTA. 30-60 seconds spoken per ad.

Companion to AIS-Live-paid-ads-hooks.md (original seven hooks).

Style rules: no em dashes, no price mentioned in ad copy, no assumed acquisition channel (say "how they land clients," not "cold email").

---

**Standard body (all ads):**

I’m hosting an online event called AIS Live where you'll learn exactly how to start an AI agency and sign your first clients, what to charge for different types of projects, and which industries are actively buying AI right now. All from practitioners doing it today, with their real numbers.  Nothing hidden, you can ask questions. And when it’s over, you’ll know exactly how to get paid, because you’ll have seen it for yourself. I’d love for you to come, consider this your invitation, and if you’d like more details, just click to see everything that’s going to be covered.

---

## **Ad 1 — The identity gap (knows AI, never been paid)**

You're the AI person in your circle, but you’re not making any money from it. Nobody knows that, right? You sound like you've got it figured out. But thxcere's this quiet gap between how you talk about AI and what's in your bank account, and it eats at you. Here's the truth: you've never seen how the money side works, because nobody films that part. Until now.

## **Ad 2 — The screenshot envy (everyone else is getting paid)**

You keep seeing other people post about how much they’re making with AI automation, and it stings a little. Because they aren’t  smarter than you. That's what makes it sting. But there is a subtle difference  you might not be aware of. People talk about how much money they’re making and what they built, but they don’t tell you how they got the client. They don’t say how long it took them. They don’t show you the contract. And those details are the difference between being smart and being paid. So to help you, I’m going to reveal everything.

## **Ad 3 — The real-or-hype torment (is any of this even real?)**

You honestly can't tell anymore if the AI money is real or if you've been falling for hype. One fear says all the videos about AI on YouTube are just noise. The other says it's real and happening without you. The only way you can know for sure is not by watching videos or reading about that, but by talking to people who are doing this in the real world and making money from it. And I’d like to introduce you. 

## **Ad 4 — The fog (can't see the first step)**

 How do you go from watching videos about AI on YouTube to people paying you for your AI expertise? What’s the first step? Is there a roadmap you can follow?  Or do you just have to wander around in the dark and figure it out? Because it feels like that’s what you’re doing right now. But what if you didn’t have to?
