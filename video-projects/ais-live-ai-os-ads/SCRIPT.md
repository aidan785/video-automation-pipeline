# Script 2: AI Just Killed Executive Assistants

Ad type: Contrarian how-to / mechanism demonstration  
Audience: People who need help staying on top of their work but do not have an executive assistant

## Spoken script

So AI just killed executive assistants.

Well, it killed their jobs, anyway.

You can now build a personal AI OS that helps organize your tasks, manage your calendar, prepare you for appointments, and keep important follow-ups from disappearing.

Here's how.

First, give it context and instructions about who you are, what you do, and what you're responsible for.

Then connect the tools where your tasks, calendar, messages, and documents already live.

Finally, give it workflows for things like planning your day, preparing for meetings, and tracking follow-ups.

Now you can tell it what you need in plain English and get help based on your actual work instead of another generic answer from a chatbot.

It doesn't need to take a break, and it never forgets anything.
