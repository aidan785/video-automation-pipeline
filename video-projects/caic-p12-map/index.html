Where consultants go wrong: three ways
1 · Pricing in your head while you map
Map first. Price later, on impact.
2 · Settling for the first call
Book the second call. It's not failure, it's the process.
3 · Taking a “simple” step at face value
“We check the file” hid ½ million PDFs. Poke every “simple.”
